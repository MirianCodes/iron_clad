# iron_clad
Team Members: Ben Layton, Mirian Ventura-Garcia, Rena Simon

Welcome to the first Playable Build, battle or spare entities and learn the mechanics with this tutorial level.

CONTROLS:
- Movement: W, A, S, D / Arrow Keys
- Sword Attack: Space Bar
- E to spare the entity
- F to interact with the wheel runestone, V to spin the wheel, K to skip
- G to move the push block puzzle (still in development)

GAMEPLAY FEATURES:
- Combat & Effects : Taking damage triggers a quick red flash and a temporary invincibility window to reduce spawn killing.
- The Guilt System: Sparing entities reduces your guilt, while high guilt tints your vision, making it harder to play the game (still in development).
- Wheel Checkpoints: Spin the wheel across your journey for random blessings (like King's Blessing or Flying Feet) or curses (like Blood Curse or Guilt Trips).
